Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the LIFE_OS Calendar Integration, a personal automation used by its owner.

Google Calendar data accessed

The integration may access Google Calendar data made available through the Google Calendar OAuth scope, including calendars, events, event times, titles, descriptions, attendees, and related calendar metadata needed to perform the requested function.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to provide LIFE_OS functions requested by the user, including private daily briefings, schedule awareness, and calendar actions explicitly requested by the user.

Processing and storage

Calendar data may be processed by the user's private LIFE_OS/Hermes automation environment and by AI or infrastructure services configured by the user solely to fulfill requested functions. Operational logs may retain limited technical metadata needed for reliability and troubleshooting. OAuth credentials are kept private and are not published on this site.

Sharing and advertising

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or made publicly available. It is not shared except with service providers or infrastructure components necessary to perform the user's requested LIFE_OS functions.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed for the user's LIFE_OS workflows and operational reliability. The user can revoke Google access at any time from their Google Account security settings and can remove locally retained LIFE_OS data from the systems they control.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
